The Joy Bundle

Planning a large gathering, a celebration, or a party with friends, family, or colleagues? You’ve come to the right room.

Choose from seven party packages. Each sitting is three hours. Drinks climb from soft drinks to the reserve pour. Food climbs with them: starters, mains, salad and staples, dessert.

Birthdays, anniversaries, offsites, farewells, retirement evenings. The kitchen, the bar, and the floor build it around your headcount. The outlet confirms the evening’s bottles before anything is printed on an invite.
